### Form

`elody:Form`: A create/edit form: the node shape whose property shapes are its fields (elody:shape), a label and a submit action. The field widgets come from shui:editor / sh:datatype / sh:class on those property shapes; nothing is repeated here (R1).

Declared under `elody:form` of [Entity Ui](./entity-ui.md#entity-ui), `elody:form` of [App](./app.md#app).
